## Observations

### 🟢 Working Well
- **Wheat unlocked turn 13** — vs NEVER in sim 5. Bumping Sowing/Withered Crop count fixed the pacing.
- **Animal Husbandry → Feast → Tavern loop** — Alice ran this TWICE to clean Discontent. The food chain works beautifully as a self-sustaining engine.
- **Racketeering is pure politics** — Charlie extorted both Alice and Bob, got cards without fighting. Players offered their worst cards to avoid Brawl. Exactly the negotiation the game wants.
- **[Labour] forced Claw draws** — Alice got Raid TWICE from Wheat access. The tax works — farming brings unrest.
- **Gathering fix validated** — didn't appear this game but its slot was taken by more useful cards (Sowing/Withered Crop), which is the point.

### 🔴 Problems
- **No culture after 30 turns** — nobody has culture! Highlander bounced for Bob, no Nomad appeared in Season. Culture cards are position 21+ in this shuffle. With 37 cards and 6 cultures, you see 4 per Season — should hit one every 2 Seasons. Bad luck this time?
- **Bob can't access Wheat** — he has Withered Crop but no Harvest in HIS discard. His Sky Dance → Rite → Worship of Fertility triggers the Harvest EVENT but no Harvest CARD goes to his discard. The distinction is important and potentially confusing.
- **Plough/AH cannibalize Pastures** — Alice lost Wheat access (via Sowing) because Animal Husbandry ate her 2nd Pasture. Farming cards destroying the land that unlocked farming is thematically cool but mechanically punishing.
- **Charlie is a Rabble bomb with no trigger** — 4 Rabble, 9 cards. Warband is still in the Claw pile. Racketeering helps but can't mass-exploit Rabble.
- **Herbalism + Culture bounce is a trap** — Bob wasted 2 cards trying to recover Highlander. Should Herbalism skip the Immediate check? Probably not — that would be too strong.
