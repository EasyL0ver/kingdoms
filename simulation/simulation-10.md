## Observations

### 🟢 Working Well

1. **Wheat 7-card display is AMAZING** — Turn 18 was the highlight: Charlie grabbed Militia + Tavern + Mill in one action, drew 3 Claw cards. Instantly had a full economy + military defense + Discontent management. The Wheat zone is now a genuine power play worth building toward.

2. **Feed the Commoners combo** — T22: Alice grabbed 2 Wheat (+ 2 Claw Discontent), then Feed the Commoners purged 3 Discontent. The cleanup card perfectly offsets the Claw tax. Elegant.

3. **Militia exists as a threat** — Charlie never used Militia but having it in Domain is a deterrent. Nobody attacked Charlie because Militia can cancel the Brawl. Defensive value without activating.

4. **Mill→Coin→Rumour pipeline** — T21: Charlie cashed Mill for a Rumour event. Farm→economy→event works.

5. **Incite from Wheat Claw tax** — T25: Alice's Wheat draw gave her Incite, which she immediately used to deploy Poach into Bob. The Claw tax from farming created an offensive opportunity. Emergent!

6. **Land Grab as Activate is better** — It sat in Alice's hand as Discontent, she purged it with Feed the Commoners. If she'd had a land-heavy Season, she could have used it. No more feel-bad whiffs.

### 🔴 Problems

1. **Poach [Rabble] has no On Brawl effect** — deployed twice (T25, T27), both times Brawl produced nothing. Poach is [Rabble] but has no combat text. It's a hunter, not a fighter. Should it gain "On Brawl — trigger Feast" or something? Or is it purely a non-combat Rabble?

2. **Empty Brawls** — T24 and T27 both fired Brawl in Domains with no On Brawl responders. Brawl without Raid/Scavenge is pointless. Need more combat Rabble density or Brawl itself needs a base effect.

3. **No culture formed AGAIN** — 3 sims in a row. Nomad bounced on T12 and nobody retrieved it. Land and culture cards keep not aligning. Solstice never fired because no Harvest hit after Bob got it.

4. **Fields depleted to 1** — After two big Wheat plays, Fields dropped to just Famine. No Harvest fired to refill. Wheat zone burns out fast with 7-card display.

5. **Crags peek is a wasted action** — T30 Charlie peeked and... that was his whole turn. It's too weak as a standalone activate. Compare to drawing 2 from Claw.

### 🟡 Concerns

1. **Wheat rush might be dominant strategy** — Charlie's T18 play (3 cards + Militia) was so strong it made everything else look bad. If you can get Wheat access, why do anything else? The 1:1 Claw tax might not be enough downside.

2. **Brawl needs a base effect** — currently does nothing without On Brawl cards. Maybe "On Brawl — defending player discards 1 card" as a baseline?

3. **Crags needs to be bundled with Claw draw** — peeking alone is never worth a full action. Maybe "Activate — look at top 3 Claw. You may then activate Claw zone." (peek + draw 2 in one action)?

## Suggested Changes

1. **Brawl base effect** — "On Brawl — the defending player discards 1 card (their choice)." Ensures Brawl always does SOMETHING even without Raid/Scavenge.
2. **Crags activate** — bundle peek with Claw draw: "Look at top 3 Claw, then draw 2." Makes it worth an action.
3. **Poach Brawl interaction** — either give it On Brawl text or accept it's non-combat Rabble (just a body for deployment/counting).
4. **Wheat refill pacing** — with 7-card display, Harvest needs to fire more often or Fields stays empty. Maybe "On Harvest — refill to 7" is too much. Consider partial refill: "refill 3 cards."
