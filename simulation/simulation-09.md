## Observations

### 🟢 Working Well

1. **FOUR BRAWLS!** More combat than all previous sims combined. Multiple pathways: Racketeering refuse (T22), Rite→Worship of War (T24, T30), Warband (T29). All three Brawl sources validated.

2. **Foray is a star** — T29: Alice got Brawled, Foray drew Highlander from Tree, she had Crags so it STAYED. Combat gave her a culture identity. Beautiful emergent moment.

3. **Solstice forced culture** — T14: Alice dropped Nomad into Charlie's Domain ignoring prerequisites. Charlie became Nomad involuntarily. Powerful diplomatic tool. Exactly as designed.

4. **Ingenuity→Coin pivot** — Both Alice (T25) and Bob (T26) drew Ingenuity, which pulled Rumour from Coin, which triggered events. Claw→Coin→event chain. Clean pivot.

5. **Crags 2+ defense creates real costs** — T24 and T29: attackers had to discard a card to push through. Not a full block, but a meaningful tax. Highlander identity = expensive to raid.

6. **Incite fired usefully** — T12: Charlie moved Marauders to Alice's Domain on arrival. First time Incite worked in any sim because he had a Rabble already.

7. **Claw draw 2 pacing** — Games move faster. Bob accumulated 9 cards by T30 but most are problems ([Discontent]). The volume creates urgency.

8. **Scavenge is the best Rabble** — fires in every Brawl, steals from discard. Consistent value. Raid is also good (forces a card gift).

### 🔴 Problems

1. **Land Grab whiffed** — T5: Season had no [Land]. It's feast-or-famine — sometimes Season has 2-3 lands, sometimes zero. Maybe needs "if none, draw 1 from Tree" fallback? Or just accept the variance.

2. **Solstice fizzled when Alice had no culture card** — T27: she'd already given away her only Nomad. Need a 2nd culture card or Solstice feels dead after you spread. Maybe Solstice should work with culture cards in Tree pile too?

3. **Wheat untouched by everyone** — Nobody accessed Wheat in 30 turns. Charlie had Withered Crop + Harvest-in-discard but never activated it. Alice had Poach for food. The Claw draw 2 change made raw Claw so productive that Wheat feels unnecessary.

4. **Bob had no culture again** — 30 turns, no land except what he got from Forage (which all went to discard). Pure Claw player with no faction identity.

5. **Herbalism timing** — T15 Charlie discarded Herbalism to get Marauders back. Clever, but then Herbalism is gone. It's a one-shot recursion unless you have another [Knowledge] to retrieve it. Fine? Or circular?

### 🟡 Concerns

1. **Wheat pacing** — In sims 5-8, Wheat unlocked around T9-19. Here? Never. Claw draw 2 makes it easy to skip farming entirely. Is Wheat optional now (only for long games)?

2. **Pasture still invisible** — No Pasture was drawn or in play all game. The Hunt limit mechanic never mattered (only 1 Poach activated, well under global limit). Pasture's identity as Hunt enabler needs a game where multiple players compete for Poach.

3. **Mine never drawn** — Coin deck barely touched (2 Rumours drawn via Ingenuity). Mine sat undrawn. Needs more Coin access to be relevant.

## Suggested Changes

1. **Accept Wheat as optional** — or add a forcing function (Domain card says "On round 5+, you must have Wheat access or discard 1"?). Or just trust that longer/bigger games push toward farming.
2. **Land Grab fallback** — "Take all [Land] from Season. If none, you may draw 1 from Tree." Prevents dead draws.
3. **Solstice wording** — consider "from your discard or from the Tree pile" for culture spread. More flexible.
4. **More games with 4+ players** to test Pasture/Hunt limit mechanics.
