- **Charlie's dual-culture discard** — both Nomad and Highlander waiting. Geography hasn't chosen him yet. Great emergent tension.

- **Bob's Rabble self-accumulation** — he drew Marauders and Scavenge, both stuck in his Domain. Without Incite (Claw position 7, about 5 draws away), he can't move them. His own fortress is becoming a liability.

- **Harvest doesn't do much** — Fields refill triggers but they're already full because nobody's taking from them. Harvest needs Wheat access to matter, creating a chicken-and-egg problem.

### 🟢 vs Previous Sims

## Observations

### 🟢 Working Well
- **Incite turn 23 was the best moment yet** — Bob went from 3 Rabble liability to weaponizing them across 2 players in 1 turn. Explosive and strategic.
- **Culture from discard** — Charlie reclaimed Nomad after 21 turns. The system works.
- **Rabble accumulation creates tension** — Alice's Domain is a ticking bomb. She knows Brawl is coming.
- **Cross-culture combat** — Nomad Alice vs Highlander Bob. Eldership CAN'T save her. Culture matters defensively.
- **Claw as double-edged sword** — Alice drew Claw hoping for Incite, got Raid instead. More Rabble! The deck punishes random draws.

### 🔴 Problems
- **WHEAT IS COMPLETELY LOCKED** — 30 turns, 3 players, zero Wheat access. Sowing needs 2+ [Nature] (Charlie has 3!) but he never drew Sowing. Withered Crop needs Harvest in YOUR discard — Charlie qualifies but never drew Withered Crop. The gateway cards are too rare or buried too deep.
- **Fields are wasted** — Plough, Famine, Plough sitting untouched for 30 turns. The entire Wheat tier is decorative. This is a serious pacing problem.
- **Harvest does nothing without Wheat** — multiple Harvests triggered, Fields always full, no On Harvest cards. Harvest is just a "move to discard" card until Wheat opens up.

### 🟡 Design Questions
- **Should Wheat be easier to access?** Maybe Domain should grant Wheat access by default (like Claw/Tree), and gateway cards (Sowing) just remove the [Labour] Claw penalty?
- **Should Sowing/Withered Crop be more common?** Currently 2 each in 35 Tree cards. Maybe 3 each?
- **Is 30 turns without economy healthy?** The game is all combat/religion/nature with zero farming. Is that intended for this tier, or is Wheat supposed to emerge earlier?
