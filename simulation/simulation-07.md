## Observations

### 🟢 Working Well

1. **Crags scry+draw is a game-changer** — Alice used it 4 times, Charlie 4 times. Every use was meaningful and strategic. Players could plan Claw strategy instead of blind gambling. This single change makes the entire Claw loop functional.

2. **Rabble deployment happened!** Turn 27 — Charlie Incited 2 Rabble into Alice's Domain. First time in 7 sims that Rabble actually moved.

3. **Chiefdom creates political plays** — Alice used Chiefdom to deflect Charlie's Raid to Bob. "Not my problem anymore." Emergent politics.

4. **Culture formed naturally** — Charlie Highlander (T15), Alice Nomad (T16). Geography-based culture works.

5. **Crags 2+ defense creates real tension** — Charlie's double-Crags makes him nearly Brawl-proof. Attackers must pay a discard tax. This is the Highlander identity: hard to raid.

6. **Feed the Commoners is a Rabble counter** — Bob instantly purged 2 enemy Rabble (T29). Creates counterplay.

7. **Brawl is IMMINENT** — Charlie has Warband + Scavenge planted in Alice's Domain. Turn 31-33 produces the first Brawl across all simulations.

### 🔴 Problems

1. **Still no actual Brawl in 30 turns** — we're right on the edge but didn't quite get there. The bottleneck was: deploy Rabble (T27) → get Brawl trigger (T30) → fire Brawl (T31+). That's a 3+ turn setup. Faster Brawl sources might be needed.

2. **Plough cost is brutal** — Alice lost Pasture (T22) which broke her Sowing [Nature] requirement AND weakened her Nomad anchor. Plough eating [Land] cards is very punishing.

3. **Feed the Commoners has no self-discard text** — it should probably say "Move to discard" like other Immediate one-shots. Currently sits dead in Domain.

4. **Bob is cultureless after 30 turns** — only land he has is Pasture ×2 but Nomad never appeared in Season. He's been farming quietly but has no faction identity.

### 🟡 Concerns

1. **Crags might be too strong** — it gives: Claw selection (scry+draw), Brawl defense (2+), [Nature] count, and culture anchor. Compare to Pasture: double-Feast (needs 2 Hunt cards, extremely situational). Crags outclasses Pasture dramatically.

2. **Claw is dominated by whoever has Crags** — Alice and Charlie cherry-picked every draw. Bob drew blind once and got a random Scavenge. The Crags player has a massive advantage.

3. **3-turn Brawl setup feels slow** — need Rabble in hand → mover (Incite/Chiefdom) → Brawl trigger. Could be compressed if some cards combined steps.

## Suggested Changes

1. **Fix Feed the Commoners** — add "Move to discard." after the effect.
2. **Buff Pasture** — currently way weaker than Crags. Maybe: "Activate — draw 1 from Tree" (simple but useful) or "Activate — look at top 3 Tree, reorder, draw 1" (mirror Crags for Tree pile).
3. **Consider a non-Crags Claw peek** — so players without Crags aren't totally blind. Maybe a Claw card that lets you scry.
