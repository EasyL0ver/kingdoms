## Observations

### 🟢 Working Well

1. **THREE BRAWLS!** All in 30 turns. Two via Rite→Worship of War, one via Racketeering refusal. Both Brawl pathways validated!

2. **Claw draw 2 creates real momentum** — Players accumulate cards fast. Alice hit 13 cards by turn 30. The [Discontent] flood creates genuine pressure to find Feast/Tavern/Feed the Commoners.

3. **Outriders works perfectly** — T19 Alice drew 3, kept Poach + Warband, ditched redundant Chiefdom. Selection without being broken (still draws from Claw, just filters 1).

4. **Pathfinding works** — T27 Charlie drew 2 Tree cards (Knowledge = 2). Scales well — with 3-4 Knowledge it would be massive. Clean mechanic.

5. **Feast chain is real** — T25: Poach→Feast→Marauders(cycle)+Share the Spoils(draw). Two Claw cards generated from one Feast. Economy is functioning.

6. **Bob's Raid is a curse** — His own Rabble was exploited in 2 of 3 Brawls. Demonstrates that [Rabble] in your own Domain is a liability, not an asset. Great design tension.

7. **Racketeering creates genuine decisions** — T22 Alice refused (Brawl better than offered card). T28 Alice took (Pasture > empty Brawl). Both choices felt correct.

8. **Crags peek is appropriately weak** — T10 gave useful intel but no card advantage. Land feels like land, not an engine.

### 🔴 Problems

1. **No culture in 30 turns** — Land and culture cards never aligned in anyone's Domain. In sim 7 culture formed by T15-16. Pure shuffle variance? Or is the 39-card Tree deck too diluted?

2. **Alice is a runaway** — 13 cards at T30. Drawing 2 per Claw action + Outriders + Feast chains = massive accumulation. But she can't use Warband (targets biggest Domain = herself). Her own size is her trap.

3. **Incite is broken as Immediate** — Fires on arrival when you likely have 0 Rabble. Should be Activate (use when ready) or have self-discard text. Currently sits dead in Domain.

4. **Bob had a miserable game** — 0 Nature for 29 turns. Got raided twice by his own Rabble. Couldn't do anything meaningful until T26 (Wheat access). Shuffle variance or systemic issue?

5. **Feed the Commoners still missing self-discard text.**

### 🟡 Concerns

1. **Rite→Worship of War is the dominant Brawl path** — 2 of 3 Brawls came this way. Racketeering worked once. Warband never fired (Alice owned both copies but was the biggest Domain). Is Warband's "biggest Domain" targeting too restrictive?

2. **Claw draw 2 might be too much card volume** — Alice ended with 13 cards after 30 turns. That's a lot. Most are Discontent. If she had Tavern/Feed the Commoners she could purge, but she never got Wheat access.

3. **Pasture is invisible** — blank card, no activate, appeared and disappeared without impact. Crags at least gave intel on T10.

## Suggested Changes

1. **Fix Incite** — change from Immediate to Activate. "Activate — move up to 3 [Rabble] from your Domain to other Domain(s). Discard Incite." One-shot but player-timed.
2. **Fix Feed the Commoners** — add "Move to discard." after effect.
3. **Warband targeting** — consider "any Domain" instead of "Domain with most cards"? Current wording makes it unusable for the biggest player.
4. **Culture density** — possibly increase Highlander/Nomad to 4 copies each, or add a card that searches for culture cards.
