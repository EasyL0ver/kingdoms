# 🏰 Kingdoms

A medieval-themed, cards-only tabletop game of political intrigue for **4–10 players**.

No board. No dice. No tokens. Just cards, cunning, and betrayal.

## What Is This?

Kingdoms is a freeform political card game inspired by the backstabbing diplomacy of Game of Thrones. Players forge alliances, claim thrones, and shatter friendships — all through cards alone. Every rule lives on a card: if it's not printed, it's not a rule.

## How It Works

- **On your turn:** activate one card you have access to.
- **Negotiate freely** — make deals, threats, and promises at any time. Nothing is binding.
- **The game ends** when any deck's draw pile is emptied, and the depleted deck determines *how* the winner is scored.

No two sessions play the same way.

## Design Philosophy

- **Cards ARE the rules** — zero rulebook needed mid-game
- **Politics first** — mechanics support, never replace, the social layer
- **Dynamic win conditions** — whichever deck empties determines scoring
- **Emergent asymmetry** — no preset factions, power shifts organically
